The Problem & The Approach

The problem

Off-the-shelf software forces a business to adapt its process to the tool. Past a certain size, that trade-off starts costing more than it saves.

The approach

We build software around your actual process — including the parts that don't fit a generic tool — and connect it to whatever else you already run through proper APIs.

FAQ

How long does a typical software project take?

Most projects run 6 to 16 weeks depending on scope. We scope in phases so you see a working result early, rather than waiting for one large release.

Do you work with early-stage businesses, or only established ones?

Both. Early-stage teams usually need one thing built well; established businesses usually need existing systems connected or replaced. The approach works the same either way.

What do you need from us to get started?

A working session on what's actually breaking or missing today, plus access to any existing systems or brand material relevant to the project.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes, most clients stay on a support or iteration arrangement after launch, since real usage is where decisions actually get tested.
