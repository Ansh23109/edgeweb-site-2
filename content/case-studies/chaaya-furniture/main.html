The Goal & The Approach

The goal

A furniture business wins or loses on two things: whether the right people find the site, and whether those visitors go on to convert. Chaaya Furniture needed both to improve together, not one at the expense of the other.

The approach

Treat SEO, paid ads and conversion as one loop instead of three separate services. SEO builds durable organic visits, ads add targeted reach where there's buying intent, and conversion work makes sure every visitor from either source has a clear path to act.

What We Do

Three workstreams, one loop.

Search Engine Optimisation

Improving how the site is found in search, so more of the right visitors arrive without paying for every click.

Ads Management

Managing paid advertising to reach people already looking for what the business sells, and adjusting as results come in.

Conversion Improvement

Working on the site itself so a higher share of visitors take the next step, whether they arrived from search or from an ad.

Measure & Iterate

Tracking what's working across all three, then putting more effort behind what earns it and less behind what doesn't.
