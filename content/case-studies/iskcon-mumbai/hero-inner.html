ISKCON Mumbai Bureau:
an internal platform
for managing training videos.

EdgeWeb built a website and a learning-video management portal for the Bureau of the International Society for Krishna Consciousness (ISKCON), Mumbai, on a full MERN stack (MongoDB, Express, React, Node.js). The engagement was delivered under NDA for the Bureau's trustees — shared here with their permission, at the level of detail they've approved.
