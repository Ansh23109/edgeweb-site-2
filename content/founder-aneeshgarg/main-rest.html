Where marketing and technology meet
The problem

Marketing and engineering often work apart.

A campaign launches without the tracking to measure it. A product ships without the positioning to sell it. Both sides end up guessing at what the other needs.

The role

Someone has to sit between both.

My part at EdgeWeb is making sure growth and engineering are talking about the same goals from day one — not reconciling two separate plans after launch.
