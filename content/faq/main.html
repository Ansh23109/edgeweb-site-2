What does EdgeWeb actually build?

Custom web and mobile applications, process automation, applied AI, cloud infrastructure and the data layer connecting them — plus the digital marketing to bring the right traffic once it's live. Most engagements combine two or three of these rather than just one.

How does a project usually start?

With a working session on what's actually breaking — not a feature list. From there we scope an architecture before writing any code, so you know what you're committing to before the build starts.

Do you work with early-stage businesses, or only established ones?

Both. Early-stage teams usually need one product built well; established businesses usually need existing systems connected or replaced. The architecture-first approach works the same either way.

What's the typical engagement length?

Most builds run 6–16 weeks depending on scope. We scope in phases so you see a working system early, rather than waiting for one large release.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes — most clients stay on a support or iteration arrangement after launch, since real usage is where architecture decisions actually get tested.

Where is EdgeWeb based, and do you work with clients outside India?

EdgeWeb is based in India, and we work with clients both locally and internationally — all delivery is remote-first with regular calls on your time zone.
