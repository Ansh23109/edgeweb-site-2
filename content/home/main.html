About EdgeWeb

Technology should remove complexity — not relocate it.

EdgeWeb is a full-stack IT solutions company based in India, working with businesses that are past the point where spreadsheets and disconnected tools hold. We design and build the applications, automation, data pipelines and infrastructure a business runs on — as one team, not a chain of vendors.

We call the resulting architecture the Edge — the point where a business's operations meet the systems that run them, engineered so the seam doesn't show.

How We Work

From business problem to running system.

Six stages, one continuous build — not six separate handoffs.

01

Business Challenge

We start with what's actually breaking — a manual process, a data gap, a system that can't scale — not a feature wishlist.

02

Architecture

We map how the pieces should connect before we write a line of code: data flow, ownership, failure points.

03

Technology

Applications and infrastructure are built against that architecture, on a stack chosen for the problem, not our preferences.

04

Automation

Manual steps between systems are replaced with logic that runs on its own — and is observable when it doesn't.

05

Intelligence

Where it earns its keep, we layer in AI — for classification, forecasting or decision support, never for its own sake.

06

Business Outcome

The system ships measured against the original problem: hours saved, errors removed, revenue unblocked.

FAQ

Questions we get before a project starts.

What does EdgeWeb actually build?

Custom web and mobile applications, process automation, applied AI, cloud infrastructure and the data layer connecting them — plus the digital marketing to bring the right traffic once it's live. Most engagements combine two or three of these rather than just one.

How does a project usually start?

With a working session on what's actually breaking — not a feature list. From there we scope an architecture before writing any code, so you know what you're committing to before the build starts.

Do you work with early-stage businesses, or only established ones?

Both. Early-stage teams usually need one product built well; established businesses usually need existing systems connected or replaced. The architecture-first approach works the same either way.

What's the typical engagement length?

Most builds run 6–16 weeks depending on scope. We scope in phases so you see a working system early, rather than waiting for one large release.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes — most clients stay on a support or iteration arrangement after launch, since real usage is where architecture decisions actually get tested.

Where is EdgeWeb based, and do you work with clients outside India?

EdgeWeb is based in India, and we work with clients both locally and internationally — all delivery is remote-first with regular calls on your time zone.
