A little about me

“I’ve spent the last 5+ years working around technology and digital products — building, solving, testing and refining what actually works.”

I’ve spent the last 5+ years working around technology and digital products — building websites, solving technical problems, working with teams, and figuring out how technology can make businesses operate better.

Over time, I became less interested in simply building websites and more interested in understanding the business behind them. That thinking eventually shaped EdgeWeb.

Why I built EdgeWeb
The problem

Businesses don’t need more technology.

They need the right technology. The kind that supports growth, reduces friction, clears operational bottlenecks, and makes the business easier to run.

The idea

Technology should solve something.

A website should generate business. An application should reduce manual work. Automation should save time. Design should make people’s decisions clearer. That is the thinking behind EdgeWeb.
